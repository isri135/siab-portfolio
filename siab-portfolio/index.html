Case study · Fitz for Ward 5

From world champion to Ward 5.

The brief

Introduce candidate Fitz Vanderpool, a former world champion boxer, to voters with a short film that felt personal rather than like a typical political ad.

The approach

A mixed-media documentary that combines archive fight footage, collage-style animation of his titles and today's footage from his gym and community.

Delivered

A 70-second campaign film that tells his story from the ring to Ward 5.

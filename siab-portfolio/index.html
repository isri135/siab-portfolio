08 · FAQ

Good questions.

How fast is turnaround?

Event reels are usually ready within 72 hours, and highlight edits the next day. Brand films take 1–3 weeks depending on scope. Rush delivery is available.

Do you travel?

Yes. Travel within [your area] is included, and further afield is quoted per project.

How do bookings and payment work?

A 50% deposit secures your date, and the balance is due on delivery. Larger projects can be split into stages.

Is music licensing included?

Yes. Every project uses properly licensed music, so your content won't be muted or taken down, even in paid ads.

Can I get the raw footage?

Yes, raw footage is available as an add-on.

What do you need from me?

Your goals, any brand guidelines, and access to the location and people. We'll take care of the rest.
